What ShiftMate UK is

ShiftMate UK is an offline-first personal rota and estimated-pay helper for UK shift workers. It runs on your device. It is not a payroll service, accountant, or HMRC product.

Data we collect

We do not require an account. In the current MVP we do not operate a backend that receives your rota or pay data.

Data you enter (job details, shift types, scheduled/worked times, leave, estimate inputs) is stored locally on your device using the app’s on-device database.

We do not sell your personal data.

Web app storage

The web version stores your data in your browser’s local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use Export to keep a backup file.

Payments

If you buy the one-off unlock, payment is handled by Stripe (web) or Google Play (Android). They process your payment details under their own privacy policies; we never see your full card details. We may receive basic purchase records (such as email, amount and date) needed for receipts, refunds and tax.

Estimates and accuracy

Pay and take-home figures shown in the app are estimates only. They are not a payslip and are not HMRC-accurate PAYE calculations. Always check your employer payslip and official sources for tax, National Insurance, and pension.

Permissions

The MVP is designed to work without special device permissions beyond normal app storage for its local database. If future versions request permissions (for example notifications or file export), we will update this policy and explain why.

Backups

If you use Android backup / device transfer features, local app data may be included in those system backups under your device/Google account settings. That is controlled by your device, not by a ShiftMate cloud service.

Children

ShiftMate UK is aimed at working adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change (for example if cloud sync is added). The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
